# Stage 11 — Crystal Cavern

World 2 opener for the **Snow Brothers 3D** remake. In the arcade original, stage 11 is the first floor after Boss 1 and introduces the underground cave stretch (stages 11–20).

## Fantasy

Nick drops into a torch-lit crystal chamber. Rock mites patrol the ledges, cave bats skim the mid-air lanes, and living crystals bounce between platforms. Same snow-and-kick loop as Stage 1 — new vertical routing and enemy mix.

## Layout goals

- Single-screen arena (classic Snow Bros rule), remapped into a shallow 3D diorama
- Three vertical bands of ledges so snowballs can chain downward
- Side pockets that punish careless jumps (soft traps, not unfair pits)
- Crystal glow + torch light as the World 2 visual signature (teal / stone / ember)

## Enemy mix (prototype)

| Kind | Role | Snow hits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rock mite | Ground / ledge patrol | 3 |
| Cave bat | Mid-height flyer | 3 |
| Living crystal | Heavier ledge walker | 4 |

## Win / fail

- **Clear:** every foe frozen and shattered via kicked snowballs (idle snowballs still count until kicked or expired after a roll)
- **Fail:** three hits from unfrozen enemies

## Controls

- Move: WASD / Arrow keys
- Jump: Space
- Throw snow: J / Z
- Kick snowball: K / X

## 3D remake notes

Keep the readable silhouette of single-screen Snow Bros: camera locked ¾ view, no free look, enemies telegraph patrol routes, kick chains are the skill expression. Stage 11 teaches “look down the cascade” before later cave floors add slopes and crushers.
